Features

Your knuckles are the new shortcut.

Small app, surprisingly clever. KnockSense lives in your menu bar and listens for one thing: you knocking on your Mac.

Knock patterns

Once, twice, three or four times. Each pattern does something different.

Knock areas

Teach it your spots: palm rest, lid, side. Knock in different places for different actions.

Any action

Play/pause, volume, lock screen, screenshots to the clipboard, Mission Control, any app, website, Shortcut, AppleScript or shell command.

Works during movies

Knocks stand out even over a loud film or music, so you can pause without hunting for the keyboard.

Ignores the noise

Doors, claps and footsteps don't count. Teach it your knock, and press “That wasn't me” to make it smarter.

Laptop Guard

Leaving your Mac? Arm it. If someone touches it, you get a photo, a siren and an alert on your phone.

One-click updates

Press “Check for Updates” and KnockSense downloads, verifies and installs the newest version by itself.

Motion sensor (beta)

On supported MacBooks, feel knocks through the built-in motion sensor, with the microphone switched off.

Get started

Up and knocking in a minute.

No account, no setup wizard. Just three steps.

Download & move

Download KnockSense, unzip it and drag KnockSense.app into your Applications folder.

Open & allow

Open it and click Allow when macOS asks for the microphone. A ✋ appears in your menu bar.

Knock twice

Knock twice next to the trackpad. Your music plays. Then make it yours in Settings.

The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't verify the developer, because KnockSense isn't distributed through the App Store. Right-click the app → Open → Open. If there's no Open button, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.
Privacy

It listens for knocks. Nothing else.

Sound is analysed on your Mac, in real time, and thrown away. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Laptop Guard photos stay in your Pictures folder; phone alerts are optional and only go to the topic you choose. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 13 Ventura or later. MacBooks work best, because knocks travel straight through the case to the built-in microphones.

Is it free?

Yes. KnockSense is free to download and use.

Will doors, claps or music set it off?

KnockSense looks for the short, sharp sound of a knuckle on the case and ignores sounds that keep going, like speech and music. Two different sounds in a row, like a door's click and slam, don't count as a pattern. For even fewer false alarms, use “Teach My Knock” and the “That wasn't me” button.

Does it drain my battery?

Barely. The knock detector is lightweight and runs on a tiny slice of one CPU core.

Why does macOS show the orange microphone dot?

macOS always shows it while any app listens to the microphone. KnockSense only analyses sound for knocks and never records it. Pause it from the menu bar at any time.

Can I still use “Hey Siri”?

macOS turns off the “Hey Siri” wake word while any app is listening to the microphone, the same as during a Zoom call. Pause KnockSense to use it, or start Siri with its keyboard shortcut.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

Apps from outside the App Store show this warning unless the developer pays for Apple notarization. KnockSense is safe to open; see the steps above.
